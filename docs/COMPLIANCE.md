# Outreach compliance notes (cold calls and email)

> This is a practical checklist, **not legal advice**. Have a lawyer review your
> outreach process before you start calling or emailing at volume.

## Phone (TCPA, Do Not Call)
- **Owner direct phones are often personal mobile numbers.** Treat them like consumer
  numbers: dial manually. No autodialers, prerecorded messages or texts without prior
  express consent.
- Check numbers against the **National Do Not Call Registry** (and your state's list, if it
  has one) before calling. Calls to business lines are generally exempt, but owner mobiles
  may not be.
- Some states have stricter "mini-TCPA" laws (e.g. Florida, Oklahoma, Maryland, Washington):
  limited calling hours, call-frequency caps and consent rules.
- If anyone asks not to be called again, set **Do not contact** in the app right away.
  Exports will flag those records.

## Email (CAN-SPAM)
- Use honest "From" names and subject lines.
- Include your company's physical postal address.
- Include a working unsubscribe link and honor opt-outs within 10 business days.
  Mark those contacts **Do not contact**.

## Data handling
- Every contact stores its **source** (e.g. Apollo) and **confidence**, so you can explain
  where a number came from if asked.
- Registered agent details come from public filings and are **not** sales contacts.
- Only you and your boss can log in. Exported CSV/Excel files contain personal data, so
  store and share them carefully.
